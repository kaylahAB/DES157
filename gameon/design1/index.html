Game On

initial design for a game on a russian roulette theme dice game (might change and add detail later)

the game is a simple dice game where players take turns rolling a die. if they roll a 6 or a 1, they lose and are out of the game. if they roll any other number, they can choose to either keep their score or risk rolling again. the goal is to have the highest score without getting blown away or rolling a 6 or rolling a 1. the rules are not fully defined yet.
