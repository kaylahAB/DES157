Dice & Roulette

One bullet. Six chambers. Roll the dice and see who is still standing. Now, it's your turn!

Play

Pull the trigger on a new game. Press Enter to choose how many players.

Rules

  • Take turns rolling two dice.
  • Snake eyes (1 and 1): you are blown away.
  • Total of 3 to 5: you are safe.
  • Total of 6 to 12: spin the gun. One shared revolver, one bullet, and every empty click makes the next spin riskier.
  • Last player standing wins!
