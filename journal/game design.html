Game Design Principles

November 17, 2026

Reading “Game Design Principles” from Juego Studio gave me words for things I have felt as a player but never named. The article lists seven principles that designers and developers should focus on:

  • clear goals
  • engaging core mechanics
  • flow
  • balance
  • feedback and rewards
  • playtesting
  • sound design

The one that stuck with me is keeping core mechanics simple. The author points out that Super Mario is built on just a few actions, like running, jumping, and collecting power-ups, and that is exactly why it is so easy to pick up. When a game is simple and easy to understand, people tend to enjoy it more than overcomplicated ones. This made me think about games I quit early and have yet to go back to. It was usually because the game was too hard or complex for me to understand, or because my attention was pulled away from what I was trying to do, which is the loss of flow and engagement the article warns about.

This connects to the GameJam project I previously worked on. During game testing, many players were confused or diverted from the main goal and wanted to do something completely unscripted. I tend to focus on visuals when designing a game, but the article made me ask whether a player would know their next move without visuals or instructions telling them. I also liked that balance does not mean easy: a game needs enough challenge to stay interesting without demanding superhuman reflexes.

The advice on playtesting, putting real people in front of the game and changing it based on what they do, is something I find enjoyable and valuable. I love watching people's reactions, seeing them get lost in the experience, or seeing them do something I never thought of. The article admits that sound design is not strictly game design, but I think music and audio cues deserve as much planning as the visuals.

Source: Game Design Principles Every Game Designer Should Know by Juego Studio

/ noun / game design: shaping goals, rules, and feedback so that play stays engaging and immersive!
